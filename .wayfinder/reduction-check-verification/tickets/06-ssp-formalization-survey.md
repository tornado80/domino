## Question

Which mechanized formalizations of state-separating proofs (or of package-based crypto games)
exist, and which of them can give the semantic stage of the theorem (advantage of the
construction ≤ advantage of the assumption, with the adversary composed with R)? Candidates:
SSProve (Rocq), Lean libraries such as VCV-io, EasyCrypt SSP work, and others. For each: the
proof assistant, composition model, how close it is to Domino's graph model, maintenance
state, and the cost of a connection to a Lean graph theorem. Write a markdown summary as an asset.
