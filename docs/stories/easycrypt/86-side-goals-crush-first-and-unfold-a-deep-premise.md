## 2. What goes wrong

### 2.1 Nothing destructs the premise

`close_side_goal` (`driver.rs`) sends the quick close `auto => /#.`, then `fallbacks()`:

| k | Sentence |
|---|---|
| 1 | `smt().` |
| 2 | `move => <binders> hpre.`, `unfold_premise(hpre)`, `smt().` |
| 3 | `smt(<hints>).` |
| 4 | `move => <binders> hpre.`, `unfold_premise(hpre)`, `smt(<hints>).` |

No step destructs the premise. After `sp k l.` the premise is a chain of equalities (`ctr0{2} =
ctr{2} /\ ec_result0{2} = None /\ …`) and, for a `rcond` side goal, nested `exists` over the
variables that `sp` overwrote. `/>` substitutes the equalities, destructs the conjunctions and the
existentials, and reduces the record projections. The experiments of §1.1 show that this is what
the solver needs.

### 2.2 The unfold of fallbacks 2 and 4 is never sent

In the 95 transcripts, fallbacks 2 and 4 go `move => &1 &2 hpre.` straight to `smt`: no `rewrite
/inv … in hpre.` The cause is in `unfold_premise`:

```rust
let mentions = self.front().is_some_and(|g| {
    g.hyps.iter().any(|h| {
        h.name == name
            && h.form.as_ref().is_some_and(|f| goals::mentions_op(f, op_leaf(&head)))
    })
});
if !mentions {
    return Ok(false);
}
```

`try_with_premise` sends `move => <binders> hpre.` with `Want::Head`. A `Head` answer cuts every
formula at depth 4 (`json.rs`, `Form`: "a node deeper than 4 is a stub: its `kind`, and its `op` for
an `app`, nothing else"). `mentions_op` stops at a stub. In a side goal after `sp`, `inv` is the 10th
conjunct of a right-nested `/\` chain (H5 ~ H6_0 Test: 9 equalities, then `inv`, `side_inv_H5`,
`side_inv_H6`), so it is at depth 9 or more. The search never reaches it, `mentions` is `false`, and
the unfold returns without sending anything. For a `rcond` side goal `inv` is also under the `exists`
binders, even deeper.

In the leaves the same function works, because there `auto => &1 &2 hpre.` has consumed the `sp`
equalities and `inv` is near the root of `hpre`. Thus the fault shows only in side goals. Fallbacks 2
and 4 are then fallbacks 1 and 3 with an extra `move`, so they can never close more than 1 and 3.

## 3. Work to do

### 3.1 A crush fallback, first

Add a side-goal fallback before today's fallback 1. On an ambient side goal `forall <binders>, A => B`
(after `reduce_to_ambient`), send

```
move=> /> <binders> *; smt().
```

with `<binders>` as `try_with_premise` finds them (`&1 &2` for a `branch-condition`, `&hr` (or the
memory that `goals::as_forall` returns) for a `side-goal`). It closes the side goal when EasyCrypt
accepts it and the goal count drops by one (`self.count() + 1 == before`, as in `try_with_premise`),
else roll back.

- `FALLBACKS` becomes 5 and the fallbacks are renumbered 1 (crush) … 5. Update `Role::SideGoalFallback`,
  `Closing::Fallback`, the live page and the report's time-by-role rows.
- If `/>` leaves several goals, the `;` sends `smt()` to each. The success test above covers that.
- A goal that is not `forall …, A => B` (no premise): skip the crush.
- Keep the quick close first (§7 Q1).

### 3.2 Unfold a deep premise

`unfold_premise` must not decide "nothing to unfold" from a cut formula.

- `goals::mentions_op` returns "maybe" at a stub: a new `goals::may_mention_op(form, name)` is `true`
  if `op_is(form, name)`, or `form.stub`, or a child may mention it. `unfold_premise` uses it.
- EasyCrypt accepts `rewrite /op in h.` when `op` does not occur in `h`; it only warns
  `unused unfold: /op` (checked on EasyCrypt of 2026-10-21). `unfold_premise` returns `Ok(false)`
  when the answer has that warning for every op, so callers see "nothing unfolded" as today.
- Unchanged: the order (all `unfold_ops`, then only the head), and that the unfold goes to the
  hypothesis `name` only.

This also changes the leaf path (`reduce_leaf`, `Step::Premise`) where a premise was deep. That is a
fix there too, but §6 checks that the hops' part fallbacks do not get slower.

## 4. Rejected alternatives

- **Clear the `side_inv` hypotheses** (`move=> &1 &2 [#] <k>? hinv _ _; smt().`). It closes the same
  guard (§1.1). But Domino must count the conjuncts before `inv` for each goal, and a guard that needs
  a game invariant loses it. Keep it for §8.
- **Make `side_inv_<C>` opaque.** Tried in §1.1; it did not help `auto => /#.`
- **Drop the `mentions` check and always send the unfold.** One extra round trip on every premise
  (each is cheap), but the ladder can no longer tell "unfolded" from "nothing to unfold" without the
  warning anyway; §3.2 reads the warning and keeps the check for formulas it can see.
- **Ask for `Want::Full` after `move => … hpre.`** Correct, but a side goal's premise is the largest
  formula of the walk (the whole `inv` and both `side_inv`s, 0.2–3 MB answers in the reports); `Head`
  was chosen for that reason (ADR 0012).

## 8. Not in this story: side goals that need a game invariant

The 49 side goals the crush leaves need instances. The H2_1 ~ H3_0 SameKey `rcondf` side goal
(`k1 = k2` for two accepted sessions with the same `sid`) closes by hand:

```
+ move => &m.
  auto => /> &hr.
  move=> ? ? ? ? ? ? ? ? hr2 ? ? ? ? ? ? ? ? hl2 hr1 hl1 _ _ _ hside hnab hs1 hs2.
  have [#] _ _ _ _ hsid hkeys hinit hacc _ _ := hside hnab.
  move: hsid hkeys hinit hacc.
  rewrite /Comp_H2.GameInv_sid__wellformed_of_H2 /Comp_H2.GameInv_keys__wellformed_of_H2
          /Comp_H2.GameInv_time__of__initialization_of_H2 /Comp_H2.GameInv_time__of__acceptance_of_H2 /=.
  move=> hsid hkeys hinit hacc.
  have := hsid ctr1{m} hs1; have := hsid ctr2{m} hs2.
  have := hkeys ctr1{m} hs1; have := hkeys ctr2{m} hs2.
  have := hinit ctr1{m} hs1; have := hinit ctr2{m} hs2.
  have := hacc ctr1{m} hs1; have := hacc ctr2{m} hs2.
  rewrite -hl1 -hl2 /=.
  smt().
```

`move=> /> &hr *; smt().` alone fails there. The general form (pick the game invariants and the keys
from cvc5's unsat core, `have := h<p> <key>`) is a separate story.
